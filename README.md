# EloScanner

**Free chess insights, analysis, online play and training.**

EloScanner is an independent chess website for exploring public chess-game statistics, studying positions and enjoying chess tools.

## Features

- **Chess Insights:** Explore Chess.com performance, time controls and recent form.
- **Chess Analysis:** Review chess positions and games.
- **Play Chess:** Challenge computer bots, including LARS.
- **Online Chess (Beta):** Play unrated 3+0 games with friends or other players.
- **Opening Explorer:** Study chess openings and Lichess opening statistics.
- **Puzzles:** Practice tactics with puzzles, Puzzle Sprint and Puzzle Survival.
- **Knight Maze:** Play a chess-themed knight-movement minigame.
