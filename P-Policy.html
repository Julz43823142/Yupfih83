1. Introduction

Welcome to EloScanner.

EloScanner is an independently operated, family-friendly chess website offering chess analysis, puzzles, leaderboards, interactive chess tools, and optional user accounts.

We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the personal information we collect and retain. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use our website, how we protect your information, and what rights you may have.

2. Privacy at a Glance

  • Accounts are optional. Many EloScanner features are available without registration.
  • Passkey registration. New users register with a username and a passkey, without an account password or personal email address.
  • Biometric information stays on your device. EloScanner does not receive or store your fingerprint or facial recognition data.
  • Recovery codes. Passkey users can generate eight one-time recovery codes.
  • Public profiles. Usernames and certain chess information may be publicly visible.
  • Private chess analysis. Personal chess analysis generally runs in your browser.
  • Limited leaderboard storage. Up to 110 ranked entries per leaderboard are retained; the top 100 are displayed.
  • No data sales. We do not sell your personal information.
  • Secure infrastructure. Cloudflare helps host and protect EloScanner.

3. Information We Process

User Accounts and Registration

Account registration is optional and may be enabled or disabled. New users register using a chosen username and a passkey. You do not need to provide a personal email address or create a password.

We process your username, account creation and update timestamps, internal account identifiers, passkey credential identifiers and public-key information, authentication sessions, and security and recovery records where applicable.

Your username, account creation date and public chess activity may appear on your profile, in the members directory, and in other public features. Avoid using real names or sensitive personal information in public usernames.

Passkeys and Biometric Authentication

EloScanner uses WebAuthn passkeys. Depending on your device or credential provider, you may confirm authentication using Face ID, a fingerprint, device PIN, screen lock, or another supported method.

EloScanner does not receive or store your facial recognition data, fingerprints, or device PIN. Such verification takes place on your device or with your credential provider. EloScanner stores the public-key credential information and related authentication metadata needed to verify a passkey. Your private cryptographic key is not stored in EloScanner's authentication database.

Passkeys may be synchronized or backed up by your device or credential provider under that provider's settings and privacy practices. Compatibility varies by device, browser, and operating system.

QR Code Login and Cross-Device Authentication

On compatible devices, you may begin login on a computer and approve it using a passkey on your phone. EloScanner processes temporary challenges, approval states, session identifiers, and security records to connect the correct request to the correct account. These requests expire and are protected against unauthorized reuse. Only approve login requests you recognize and initiated.

Recovery Codes and Account Recovery

Passkey users may generate eight one-time recovery codes. Codes are displayed when generated; EloScanner stores cryptographically protected representations rather than readable codes. Keep your codes private and secure.

Recovery can invalidate previous passkeys, sessions, and recovery codes. New recovery codes may need to be generated afterward. If you lose all your passkeys and valid recovery codes, EloScanner may be unable to restore access. Email-based recovery is not currently available for passkey accounts.

Authentication Sessions

We use essential secure authentication cookies to maintain sessions. Sessions may remain valid for up to 30 days unless revoked or expired earlier. We may process session identifiers, expiry information, and limited security metadata. Signing out ends the current session; certain security changes can revoke additional sessions.

Public User Profiles

Public profile pages may display your username, account creation date, profile information, chess ratings and results, and account-linked leaderboard activity. Features involving friends, challenges, game history, and online status may expose related information depending on the feature.

Account Security and Abuse Prevention

We use Cloudflare Turnstile, authentication limits, WebAuthn challenges, and other security controls. Temporary pseudonymous security counters may be derived from source IP addresses, usernames, or request information using keyed cryptographic processing. These records expire according to their purpose and may be cleaned up during later activity. Pseudonymous identifiers may still be personal data under applicable law. Cloudflare may separately process request and security logs.

Chess Usernames and Analysis

When you enter a Chess.com username, EloScanner retrieves publicly available chess information, including ratings, games, moves, opponents, and performance statistics. Personal analysis generally runs in your browser; we do not maintain a server-side archive of visitors' personal analysis reports. We do not require your Chess.com password or access private account information.

Puzzle Leaderboards

For score submissions, we process your chosen nickname or account username, score, mode, submission time, and an account identifier where linked to an account. Up to 110 ranked entries are retained per leaderboard; the top 100 are public. Entries outside the retained rankings may be removed during cleanup. Visitors may report inappropriate names for review.

Technical Information

Cloudflare may process IP addresses, request timestamps, requested pages, HTTP metadata, browser and device information, and security events to deliver and protect the site. EloScanner does not maintain a separate application database containing a complete history of every visitor's HTTP requests.

Email Communications

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you send, which we use to respond to inquiries and privacy requests.

4. Online Multiplayer & Fair Play

EloScanner allows users to play chess online with registered accounts, temporary nicknames, or anonymously.

To provide multiplayer services, we may process usernames, IP addresses, session data, chess moves, game results, ratings, and connection status.

Registered users can add friends, send challenges, share game invitation links, and view other members' public profiles and online status.

Game history and ratings may be publicly visible. Closed accounts may display an "Account Closed" status. In the future, accounts closed for confirmed Fair Play violations may display "Account Closed – Fair Play Violation."

Game and technical data may be used to maintain fair play, prevent cheating, and protect our services. Personal data is retained only as necessary and handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

5. Purposes and Legal Bases

We process information to provide chess analysis, puzzles, leaderboards, online multiplayer, optional accounts, passkey authentication, QR login, recovery codes, sessions, public profiles, friends and challenges, and to prevent spam, cheating, unauthorized access and other abuse. We also process information to maintain the service, answer inquiries, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where the GDPR applies, legal bases may include performance of a contract for requested services, legitimate interests for proportionate security and abuse prevention, consent where required for optional activities, and legal obligations where applicable.

6. Hosting, Storage and Data Retention

EloScanner uses Cloudflare for hosting, website delivery, security, and database infrastructure. Cloudflare security logs, infrastructure records, and backups may have separate retention periods.

  • Account information: generally retained while the account exists, subject to legal requirements.
  • Passkey credentials: retained while registered, unless removed or revoked.
  • Recovery codes: protected records are retained until used, replaced, revoked, or removed with the account.
  • Login sessions: valid for up to 30 days; expired records may remain until cleanup.
  • Authentication challenges and security counters: temporary records expire according to their purpose and may be removed during subsequent cleanup.
  • Chess analysis: personal reports generally remain in the browser rather than on our servers.
  • Puzzle leaderboards: up to 110 ranked entries per leaderboard; lower entries may be removed during cleanup.
  • Browser storage: cached chess data and interface preferences may remain until cleared or expired.
  • Email correspondence: retained as reasonably necessary to handle requests or legal obligations.

Cloudflare D1 database backups may temporarily contain information previously removed from the active database.

7. Cookies and Browser Storage

EloScanner does not require advertising cookies to provide its chess analysis features.

Some website functionality may use temporary browser storage.

When you sign in to an EloScanner account, we use essential authentication cookies to maintain your session.

Authentication cookies are configured with security protections including Secure, HttpOnly, and SameSite attributes.

These cookies are used for authentication and security, not for targeted advertising.

Cloudflare may also use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website security, bot detection, and normal operation.

You can manage cookies and locally stored information through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent account login from functioning correctly.

If we introduce non-essential tracking that requires consent, we will provide appropriate notice and choices.

8. External Services and Information Sharing

We use external services to operate EloScanner, including:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, database infrastructure, website security, and Turnstile.
  • Chess.com: publicly available chess information.
  • Google: email communications and certain submitted reports.
  • Device and passkey providers: Apple, Google, Microsoft, or other credential managers may manage or synchronize passkeys according to their own privacy practices.

These providers may process relevant information under their own privacy practices. Some processing may occur outside the European Economic Area, subject to applicable data protection requirements. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers for targeted advertising.

9. Account Deletion and Your Privacy Rights

Account Deletion

You can request account deletion through supported account settings or by contacting us. To protect against unauthorized deletion, we verify that you control the account, which may involve recent successful passkey authentication.

When an account is deleted, associated credentials and authentication sessions are removed from the active authentication database. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or where retention is legally required. Deleting an account does not necessarily remove unrelated anonymous leaderboard entries, independently maintained chess records, or information held by external services.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, request portability where applicable, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Contact eloscanner.contact@gmail.com to exercise your rights. We may request reasonable verification to protect your information. We respond in accordance with applicable law. You may also lodge a complaint with your relevant data protection supervisory authority.

10. Security and Children's Privacy

EloScanner is a family-friendly chess website. Creating an account is optional and does not require a personal email address. Usernames, public profiles, chess results, and certain multiplayer activity may be visible to others. Younger users should avoid sharing real names, school information, contact details, or other identifying information publicly.

We aim to minimize unnecessary personal information. Where applicable law requires parental authorization or additional protections for children, those requirements apply. Parents and guardians may contact us with concerns or requests.

Security measures include HTTPS, passkeys, secure cookies, rate limits, Cloudflare Turnstile, recovery safeguards, and restricted administrative access. No online service can guarantee absolute security.
