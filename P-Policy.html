1. Introduction

Welcome to EloScanner.

EloScanner is an independently operated, family-friendly chess website offering chess analysis, puzzles, leaderboards, interactive chess tools, and optional user accounts.

We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the personal information we collect and retain. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use our website, how we protect your information, and what rights you may have.

2. Privacy at a Glance

  • No account required. You can use EloScanner's chess analysis and other publicly available features without creating an account.
  • Optional user accounts. You can register with a username and password when registration is available.
  • Protected passwords. Passwords are not stored in readable form.
  • Public profiles. Registered users have public profiles displaying their usernames and account creation dates.
  • Private chess analysis. Personal chess analysis runs in your browser. We do not store visitors' personal analysis reports on our servers.
  • Public chess information. Our analysis tools use publicly available chess data.
  • Limited leaderboard storage. Up to 110 ranked nicknames and scores are retained per leaderboard, with only the top 100 displayed publicly.
  • No data sales. We do not sell your personal information.
  • Secure infrastructure. Cloudflare helps deliver and protect our website.

3. Information We Process

User Accounts

EloScanner offers optional user accounts. Account registration may be enabled or disabled depending on the availability of registration.

When you create an account, we process:

  • Your chosen username.
  • Your account creation date and related timestamps.
  • Internal account identifiers.
  • Password verification data.
  • Authentication sessions.
  • Limited security-related information necessary to protect accounts.

Passwords and Authentication

Passwords are processed for authentication but are not stored in plain text.

EloScanner protects passwords using salted and peppered Argon2id password hashing. Authentication information is stored in our Cloudflare D1 authentication database.

We use secure authentication cookies to maintain login sessions. Sessions have a maximum lifetime of seven days.

Users can change their passwords, sign out, and delete their accounts through the account interface.

There is currently no email-based password recovery or recovery-code service. Users should choose a unique, secure password and keep it confidential.

Public User Profiles

Registered EloScanner accounts have public profile pages.

The following information may be visible to other visitors:

  • Your username.
  • Your account creation date.
  • Your public profile page.
  • Your appearance in the members directory.

EloScanner is also developing profile sections for chess ratings, puzzle ratings, personal records, friends lists, and game history.

These sections do not necessarily mean that the corresponding information is currently collected or stored in connection with your account. Before additional account-linked information becomes publicly available, we will update this policy where necessary.

Because usernames are public, we recommend avoiding real names or sensitive personal information in your username.

Account Security and Abuse Prevention

To protect accounts against unauthorized access, automated registration, and abusive login attempts, EloScanner uses security controls such as authentication attempt limits and Cloudflare Turnstile.

Our authentication system maintains temporary pseudonymous security counters derived from attempted usernames and source IP addresses. These identifiers are protected using keyed cryptographic processing rather than being stored as plain IP addresses in the authentication counter database.

These counters expire within ten minutes and are cleaned up during subsequent account activity. Pseudonymous identifiers may still constitute personal data under applicable privacy law.

Cloudflare may separately process request information and security logs under its applicable privacy practices.

Password Safety Checks

When a new password is set, EloScanner checks it against the Have I Been Pwned password range service.

Only the first five characters of a SHA-1 lookup hash are sent to this service. The password itself and its complete lookup hash are not transmitted.

SHA-1 is not used to store account passwords.

Chess Usernames and Analysis

When you enter a Chess.com username, EloScanner retrieves publicly available information, such as ratings, games, moves, opponents, and performance statistics.

This information is used to generate your requested analysis in your browser.

We do not require your Chess.com password, access private account information, or store visitors' personal analysis reports on our servers.

Featured analyses of Magnus Carlsen and Hikaru Nakamura are also generated using publicly available chess information.

Puzzle Leaderboards

If you choose to submit a puzzle score, we process your chosen nickname, score, puzzle mode, and submission time.

The leaderboard retains the highest score per nickname and up to 110 ranked nicknames per leaderboard. Only the top 100 are displayed publicly. The remaining ten places serve as reserves and may become visible if higher-ranked entries are removed.

Entries falling outside the retained rankings are automatically removed during leaderboard cleanup.

Visitors can report inappropriate leaderboard nicknames for manual review.

When you voluntarily publish a puzzle score while signed in, you can choose to display your account username instead of a nickname. Leaderboard entries indicate whether a score was published under an account or a nickname. Account-linked submissions are associated with your account identifier; ordinary nickname submissions are not linked to an account by default.

Please avoid using your real name or sharing sensitive information in public nicknames.

Technical Information

When you access EloScanner, our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may process:

  • Request dates and times.
  • HTTP request and response details.
  • Requested pages and resources.
  • Browser and device information.
  • IP addresses and security-related information.

This processing supports website delivery, performance, and security.

EloScanner does not maintain a separate application database of complete HTTP request logs, visitor IP addresses, or browser details.

Email Communications

If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message.

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, handle privacy requests, and resolve relevant concerns.

4. Online Multiplayer & Fair Play

To provide multiplayer services, we may process usernames, IP addresses, session data, chess moves, game results, ratings, and connection status.

Registered users can add friends, send challenges, share game invitation links, and view other members' public profiles and online status.

Game history and ratings may be publicly visible. Closed accounts may display an "Account Closed" status. In the future, accounts closed for confirmed Fair Play violations may display "Account Closed – Fair Play Violation."

Game and technical data may be used to maintain fair play, prevent cheating, and protect our services. Personal data is retained only as necessary and handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

6. Hosting, Storage and Data Retention

EloScanner uses Cloudflare for hosting, website delivery, security, and database infrastructure.

For applicable Cloudflare security analytics services, relevant records may be retained for up to 31 days. Other services and technical records may have different retention periods.

Our application-specific retention practices include:

  • Chess analysis: personal analysis reports are generated in the browser and are not stored on our servers.
  • Puzzle leaderboards: up to 110 ranked nicknames per leaderboard are retained. Entries outside the retained rankings are removed during cleanup.
  • Registered accounts: account information is generally retained while the account exists.
  • Authentication sessions: sessions expire after a maximum of seven days. Expired records may remain until subsequent cleanup.
  • Security counters: authentication abuse counters expire within ten minutes and are cleaned up during subsequent account requests.
  • Browser storage: temporary information, including cached public chess data, may remain on your device until cleared or expired.
  • Email correspondence: messages are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, resolve concerns, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Database backups and Cloudflare infrastructure logs may have separate retention periods.

7. Cookies and Browser Storage

EloScanner does not require advertising cookies to provide its chess analysis features.

Some website functionality may use temporary browser storage.

When you sign in to an EloScanner account, we use essential authentication cookies to maintain your session.

Authentication cookies are configured with security protections including Secure, HttpOnly, and SameSite attributes.

These cookies are used for authentication and security, not for targeted advertising.

Cloudflare may also use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website security, bot detection, and normal operation.

You can manage cookies and locally stored information through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent account login from functioning correctly.

If we introduce non-essential tracking that requires consent, we will provide appropriate notice and choices.

9. Account Deletion and Your Privacy Rights

Account Deletion

Registered users can initiate account deletion through their EloScanner account settings.

Account deletion requires confirmation of the current password to help prevent unauthorized requests.

When an account is deleted, the associated account credentials and authentication sessions are removed from the active authentication database.

Certain information may remain temporarily in backups or where retention is required by applicable law.

Deleting an account does not automatically remove unrelated anonymous leaderboard entries or information independently maintained by external chess platforms.

You can request removal of information relating to you without providing a reason.

To exercise your rights, contact eloscanner.contact@gmail.com and identify the relevant information.

We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection supervisory authority.

We can address information controlled by EloScanner, but cannot directly delete information independently maintained by external chess platforms.
