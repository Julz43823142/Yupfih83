1. Introduction

Welcome to EloScanner.

EloScanner is an independently operated, family-friendly chess website offering chess analysis, puzzles, leaderboards, and interactive chess tools.

We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the personal information we collect and retain. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use our website and what rights you may have.

2. Privacy at a Glance

  • No account required. You can use EloScanner without creating an account.
  • Private chess analysis. Personal chess analysis runs in your browser. We do not store visitors' analysis reports on our servers.
  • Public chess information. Our analysis tools use publicly available chess data.
  • Limited leaderboard storage. Up to 110 ranked nicknames and scores are retained per leaderboard, with only the top 100 displayed publicly.
  • No data sales. We do not sell your personal information.
  • Secure infrastructure. Cloudflare helps deliver and protect our website.

3. Information We Process

Accounts — closed testing

Public account registration is closed. Operator-provisioned test accounts use a username and password; no real email address is requested. Our authentication database contains an internal, non-deliverable identifier in the email field required by the authentication software. It is not a contact address.

We store usernames, account timestamps, salted and peppered Argon2id password hashes and login sessions in our Cloudflare D1 authentication database. Passwords are processed to verify login but are not stored in readable form or intentionally logged. Accounts remain until deleted. Sessions expire after at most seven days; expired records are cleaned up during subsequent account activity. Deletion removes the account and its sessions from the active database; Cloudflare backup retention may delay removal from backups.

To protect accounts, we keep keyed, pseudonymous identifiers derived from the attempted username and source IP, with attempt counts. Counters expire within ten minutes and are cleaned up during subsequent account requests. These identifiers are not anonymous. Cloudflare may separately process request and security logs under its own applicable terms. Where a bot check is needed, Cloudflare Turnstile processes technical information to verify the request.

When a new password is set, the server checks the Have I Been Pwned password range service using only the first five characters of a SHA-1 lookup hash. The password and full hash are not sent to that service. SHA-1 is not used for stored account passwords. This check fails closed if unavailable.

Account processing supports the requested account service; proportionate abuse prevention supports our legitimate interest in securing it. You can delete a test account from its account page after confirming your password. There is currently no email recovery, recovery code or manual password-reset service. Keep your unique password securely. Existing public leaderboard nicknames are separate from accounts.

Chess Usernames and Analysis

When you enter a Chess.com username, EloScanner retrieves publicly available information, such as ratings, games, moves, opponents, and performance statistics.

This information is used to generate your requested analysis in your browser. We do not require your Chess.com password, access private account information, or store visitors' personal analysis reports on our servers.

Featured analyses of Magnus Carlsen and Hikaru Nakamura are also generated using publicly available chess information.

Puzzle Leaderboards

If you choose to submit a puzzle score, we process your chosen nickname, score, puzzle mode, and submission time.

The leaderboard retains the highest score per nickname and up to 110 ranked nicknames per leaderboard. Only the top 100 are displayed publicly. The remaining ten places serve as reserves and may become visible if higher-ranked entries are removed.

Entries falling outside the retained rankings are automatically removed during leaderboard cleanup.

Visitors can report inappropriate leaderboard nicknames for manual review.

Please avoid using your real name or sharing sensitive information in public nicknames.

Technical Information

When you access EloScanner, our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may process:

  • Request dates and times.
  • HTTP request and response details.
  • Requested pages and resources.
  • Browser and device information.
  • IP addresses and security-related information.

This processing supports website delivery, performance, and security.

EloScanner does not maintain a separate application database of visitor IP addresses, browser details, or complete HTTP request logs.

Email Communications

If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use it to respond to your message and handle relevant requests.

5. Hosting, Storage and Retention

EloScanner uses Cloudflare for hosting, website delivery, and security.

For applicable Cloudflare security analytics services, relevant records may be retained for up to 31 days. Other services and technical records may have different retention periods. This does not mean every record processed by Cloudflare is automatically deleted after 31 days.

Our application-specific retention practices are:

  • Chess analysis: reports are generated in the browser and are not stored on our servers.
  • Puzzle leaderboards: up to 110 ranked nicknames per leaderboard are retained. Entries outside these rankings are removed through automatic cleanup.
  • Browser storage: temporary information, including cached public profile data, may remain on your device until cleared or expired.
  • Email correspondence: messages are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, resolve concerns, or meet applicable legal requirements.

6. Cookies and Browser Storage

Some website functionality may use temporary browser storage. Cloudflare may also use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for security and website operation.

You can manage cookies and locally stored information through your browser settings.

If we introduce non-essential tracking that requires consent, we will provide appropriate notice and choices.
